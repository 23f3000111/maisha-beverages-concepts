All brand material belongs to Maisha Beverages Limited (HOLA). It is used here to pitch a website to the brand owner.

## Client and brand material
- **Client files (`Images/`)**: studio bottle shots and the HOLA logo on white. The flat logo (`logo/hola-logo-flat.webp`) is that file with the white background lifted off; nothing was redrawn.
- **hola.co.ke**: transparent bottle cutouts (`bottles/`), the logo with its orange glow (`logo/hola-logo*.webp`), the favicon and all flavour descriptions. `bottles/energy-red.webp` and `bottles/energy-green.webp` were cut from the Energy Red and Energy Green banners using the outline of the Energy bottle cutout.
- **Google Maps listing photo** for Maisha Beverages Limited: the six-flavour studio collage, split into `studio/orange|rose|limao|berry|cola|mojito.webp`.
- **HOLA Facebook page** (facebook.com/holabeverages): lifestyle photos in `life/`, and `studio/mango.webp`, `studio/energy.webp`.
- **YouTube** video F2DqzdPeuTY, "Maisha Beverages: Refreshing Kenyans with World Class Beverages at an Affordable Price": its thumbnail is the video poster.
